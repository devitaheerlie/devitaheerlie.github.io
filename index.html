01 — About

Two ways of looking at the same organism

My research centers on giant virus metagenome-assembled genomes (GVMAGs) and NCLDV genomics — reconstructing and classifying organisms most tools weren't built to see. Alongside that, I illustrate the microbial and marine life I study, translating sequence data and specimens into images.
